IMPORTANT SIX-AXIS WARNING:
Six-axis mode is only recommended when the video/screen has good lighting, a clear main subject, and a clean selected region. If the picture is unclear, use L0 or Log only preview first.

Before your first real session, use measurement mode to save upper and lower limits that fit your own device and comfort range. These limits are saved locally. If output works normally next time, do not restore defaults again unless you want to reset everything.

Tip: hover your mouse over buttons, sliders, and parameter names. Small tooltips explain what each item does.

With a device:
1. Double-click OSR6-Realtime-Screen.exe, or double-click Start.cmd.
2. On first run, click the restore-defaults button once and confirm.
3. In the left device panel, choose USB serial or BLE.
4. Click connect and confirm the app shows a connected status.
5. Use measurement mode to move L0 manually.
6. Save a comfortable safe upper limit.
7. Save a comfortable safe lower limit.
8. Click realtime output.
9. In the popup, choose Screen mode.
10. Choose L0 first, then try Six Axis only when the picture is clear and stable.
11. Select the screen region to read.
12. Use the recommended hybrid analysis mode first.
13. Start with the middle preset and increase intensity only after it feels stable.

Preview without a device:
1. Set output to Log only.
2. Click Show Preview.
3. Open a moving video or page.
4. Click realtime output.
5. Select Screen and L0, then choose the moving region.
6. Watch the curve and preview. Log only will not control hardware.
